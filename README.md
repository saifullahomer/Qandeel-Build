## How to install

1. Open the download link above on your Android phone.
2. Open the downloaded `qandeel.apk`. If your phone asks, allow installs from your browser.
3. Tap **Install**, then open Qandeel.

This is a test build. A new version appears here automatically whenever the app is updated; reinstalling from the same link keeps your data.
